FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should I book catering?

For most gatherings we recommend 7–14 days’ notice. Weddings and large corporate events are best reserved 3–4 weeks ahead.

Do you accommodate dietary preferences?

Yes. We prepare vegetarian, pescatarian, and spice-customized dishes. Share allergies when you inquire and we’ll guide the menu.

Is everything Halal?

Mai Colachi is committed to Halal excellence across our dining and catering menus.

Do you offer tasting menus for weddings?

Complimentary tastings can be arranged for confirmed wedding packages so you can refine flavors with our culinary team.

Can you provide staff and full-service setups?

Yes — from drop-off trays to full-service buffet and plated service with attendants.
