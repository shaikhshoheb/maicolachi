About Mai Colachi

Our story in every spice

Restaurant Story

A culinary home for Sugar Land

Mai Colachi was created to celebrate the eclectic spirit of South Asia’s culinary heritage — from Mughal-inspired feasts to charcoal-grilled street classics — served in an intimate, upscale setting.

Guests come for Chicken Malai Boti and Lahori Fish, and stay for the warmth of a room designed for conversation, celebration, and return visits.

Purpose

Mission & Vision

Two promises that guide every plate we serve and every event we host.

Mission

Flavor with integrity

To serve exceptional Halal Indo-Pakistani cuisine with consistency, generosity, and respect for tradition — whether at our tables or yours.

Vision

The landmark for celebration dining

To be Houston metro’s most trusted destination for refined Halal dining and full-service catering — where every event feels personal and every plate feels memorable.

Chef Introduction

Masters of flame & spice

Our culinary team trains in classic Indo-Pakistani technique — from dum biryani to wok-fired karahi — with an eye for presentation suited to modern dining.

Led by chefs who understand spice balance from mild to bold, every dish is finished with the same care you would expect at a celebration table.
